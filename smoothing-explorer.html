Key Terms

SES (Simple Exponential Smoothing)

A forecasting method that blends the last actual value with the previous forecast. Assigns exponentially decreasing weights to older observations.

Alpha (α) — Smoothing Constant

Controls reactivity. High α (near 1) trusts recent data more. Low α (near 0) trusts history more. Range: 0 < α < 1.

MAPE (Mean Absolute Percentage Error)

Average of |Actual − Forecast| / Actual × 100. Lower is better. Industry standard for tourism forecasting.

Naive Forecast

The simplest possible baseline. For seasonal data: same month last year. For non-seasonal data: last month's value. Any method must beat this to justify its complexity.
