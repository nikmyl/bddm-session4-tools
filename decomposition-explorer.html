Key Terms

CMA (Centred Moving Average)

Average of W consecutive months, centred on the middle month. Smooths out seasonal spikes to reveal the trend.

Seasonal Index

How much a calendar month typically deviates from the trend, in euros. Positive = above trend, negative = below. The 12 indices sum to zero.

Additive Decomposition

Actual = Trend + Seasonal + Irregular. Works when the seasonal swing stays roughly the same size regardless of the trend level.
